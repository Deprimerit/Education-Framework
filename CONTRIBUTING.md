# Contributing to the Model Constitution

We welcome scholarly critique, editorial improvements, and edge-case challenges.

## Ways to Contribute
- **Methodological & Conceptual Critique:** Please open a thread in [Discussions](https://github.com/Deprimerit/Education-Framework/discussions).
- **Drafting & Typographical Corrections:** Submit a Pull Request targeting the specific Article or rationale section.
- **Grounding:** Where possible, reference established empirical literature or international policy precedents when suggesting structural modifications.
